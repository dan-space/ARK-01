A New Home

📖 ARK-01 Mission Manual

▸ Passage policy: set a fictional ticket price for 1,000 berths. The price changes the manifest's technology, supply demand and social risk.

▸ Pre-launch: allocate exactly 24 units across Supplies, Energy, Technology and Social Stability.

▸ The voyage: scan two signals, choose a route, decide whether to land, then return to ARK-01 for the next jump.

▸ Four resources: protect Supplies, Energy, Technology and Social Stability. If any reaches zero, the escape mission ends.

▸ Three specialists: Elena handles biology and medicine; Alex handles engineering and mining; Marcus handles piloting and scanning. Click a crew card or drag it onto the mission target.

▸ Build the ark: recovered components must be installed on compatible hardpoints directly on the cinematic ARK-01 image. Every module grants a direct gameplay benefit.

▸ Win: confirm liquid water, a workable atmosphere and a stable biosphere, then bring the crew and DNA archive safely down to New Earth.
